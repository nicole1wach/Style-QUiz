Style identity quiz

Answer 12 questions about what you actually wear and how you dress. Based on your responses, you will be assigned a style identity — replicating how a recommendation algorithm might categorise you from your behaviour.

This is part of academic research for a bachelor thesis on algorithmic influence and identity in Gen Z fashion. All responses are anonymous.
